Wyckoff Guide

A compact reference for the methodology used by the assistant and stock analyzer.

Three Laws

Supply and demand drive direction, cause and effect links trading ranges to future moves, and effort versus result reveals hidden strength or weakness.

Accumulation

Look for selling climax, automatic rally, secondary test, spring, sign of strength, and last point of support.

Distribution

Watch buying climax, automatic reaction, upthrust, sign of weakness, and last point of supply.

Trading Discipline

  • Use volume to confirm price intent.
  • Prefer entries after tests, not during emotional climax bars.
  • Place invalidation near the structure that justified the trade.
